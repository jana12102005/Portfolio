Introduction

About Me.

I didn't get the branch everyone fights for — no CSE, no AI&DS. I got Biotechnology. But I made one decision early: my department won't define my ceiling.

While studying life sciences, I self-taught Python, machine learning, deep learning, web development and bioinformatics — and built real tools. Not tutorials. Systems that work.

That drive took me to IISc Bengaluru as a Research Intern under Dr. K. Sekar, where I worked on Alzheimer's detection from gene expression data — one of India's most prestigious research institutions.

I also founded New Star Infosis (NSIS) — a tech startup delivering AI, ML, and web solutions. And I lead InnoSphere, the innovation club at Paavai Engineering College.

My mission: be at the exact intersection of biology and artificial intelligence — where a protein becomes a dataset, and a disease becomes a solvable problem.
